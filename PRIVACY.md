# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Author & Data Controller:** hxni  

---

### 1. Overview & Core Philosophy
We believe your private moments, confessions of love, and playful messages belong exclusively to you and your loved ones. This interactive web application is designed from the ground up to be **100% private, client-side, and tracker-free**.

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or IP addresses.
- **No Message Storage:** When you personalize a question (e.g. `to=Sarah`), the name and message are **never stored on any backend server or database**.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** We do not use Google Analytics, advertising tracking pixels, third-party cookies, or behavioral beacons.
- **No Third-Party Data Sharing:** Your data is never sold, rented, monetized, or shared with third parties.

---

### 3. How Data Is Processed
- **Client-Side URL Parameters:** When you create or open a personalized link (e.g., `?q=will-you-be-my-valentine&to=Babe`), the parameters are parsed directly inside your own web browser. No network server records or logs the recipient’s name.
- **Local Storage (`localStorage`):** The application may use your browser's local storage solely to remember client-side preferences (such as whether audio is muted or enabled). This information stays strictly on your local device.
- **Synthesized Audio:** All sound effects are synthesized in real-time on your computer or phone using the browser's native Web Audio API without streaming external audio files or sending audio requests over the network.
- **Local Assets:** All animations, icons, and GIF illustrations are hosted locally within the application package, eliminating external CDN tracking.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
This application is completely family-friendly, does not solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or any age), and contains no age-restricted content or covert tracking.

---

### 5. Third-Party Links & Sharing
When utilizing external sharing links (such as sharing to WhatsApp, Telegram, or system share sheets), interaction with those platforms is governed by the respective privacy policies of those services.

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect feature improvements. For questions or concerns regarding privacy practices, please contact the author **hxni**.
